2. What Kinsu is and isn't

What the app does

  • Medicine reminders at the times you set, around your meals or at a time of your own.
  • Readings and logs: blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, pulse, SpO2 and temperature, plus sleep, mood, symptoms, diet, exercise and illness episodes.
  • A records vault for prescriptions, lab reports, scans and discharge summaries. You can keep records for family members too, labelled with whose they are, on your phone (section 4).
  • SOS and Medical ID: your name, blood group and health goals, public emergency numbers, and your own emergency contacts.
  • Care routines for hair, skin and eye care, with reminders and general tips.
  • Export: save the categories of records you choose as files on your phone.

Charts and summaries in the app are calculated from what you enter. If an entry is wrong, they will be too. Kinsu does not check whether a medicine, dose or schedule is right for you. It reminds you of what you entered.

What it isn't

Kinsu Health is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a doctor or other qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Kinsu does not replace your doctor, your pharmacist or your prescription. Follow your doctor's instructions about your medicines.

In an emergency, call 112. The SOS screen only opens your phone's dialler with the number ready, and you still have to press call. Kinsu does not call, message or alert anyone for you, and does not share your location.

5. Your data

Your health data is yours. In short:

  • Of what you keep in Kinsu, only two things leave your phone: your mobile number, sent to Google Firebase so it can be verified, and an encrypted copy of your recovery key, stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Mumbai. That key copy is useless without your 12-word code. Signing in also sends Firebase the technical data described in section 4 of the Privacy Policy.
  • We run no analytics, show no ads, and sell nothing to anyone.

The full details are in the Privacy Policy, which forms part of these terms. If the two ever seem to differ about how your data is handled, the Privacy Policy applies.

There is no backup

Kinsu keeps no copy of your records anywhere else. There is no cloud backup, and Android's own backup of the app is switched off. That keeps your records private, but it means:

  • If your phone is lost, stolen, broken or reset, or you uninstall Kinsu, you lose the records on it. Neither you nor we can recover them from anywhere else.
  • Your recovery code gets you back into your records on the same phone if its security settings change. It does not move records to a new phone.
  • If you lose your recovery code and your phone's security settings then change, you will not be able to open your records again. There is no master key.

Copies you take out

Exports (CSV and JSON files) and documents you save are written to your phone's Downloads folder without encryption, so that you and other apps can open them. Once a file leaves Kinsu, keeping it safe is up to you. An export is a copy for you to read and keep. Kinsu cannot restore records from it.
