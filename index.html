What Kinsu does

The file, the medicines and the readings, in one place.

Records

Every report, where you can find it.

Add prescriptions, lab reports, scans and discharge summaries with your camera, from your gallery, or as a file. Crop a photo to the page, open PDFs inside the app, and search by name, doctor or tag.

More about health records

Medicines

Medicines around your meals, the way your doctor said.

Set each medicine for an empty stomach, before or after breakfast, lunch or dinner, bedtime, or a time of your own. Daily, on the days you choose, monthly, or only when needed.

Mark a dose taken straight from the reminder. By default the reminder only says a dose is due, not which medicine, so nothing sensitive shows on your lock screen.

More about medicine reminders

Readings

Readings, and how the week went.

Log blood pressure, sugar, weight, pulse, SpO2 and temperature, and see the week on a simple chart. Note sleep, mood, symptoms, diet, exercise and illness episodes alongside them.

Private by design

What stays on your phone, and what doesn’t.

Stays on your phone

Everything you add, encrypted (AES-256):

  • prescriptions, reports, scans and their photos or PDFs
  • medicines, doses and reminders
  • readings, symptoms, sleep, mood, diet and exercise
  • the family members you add, and the records you keep for them
  • your Medical ID and emergency contacts

Leaves it

Two things, both to Google Firebase:

  • your mobile number, so it can text you a sign-in code
  • an encrypted copy of your recovery key, which opens only with the 12-word code you write down at setup

Signing in also sends Firebase the technical details it needs to sign you in safely, such as your IP address.

The honest list

What Kinsu doesn’t do.

Most apps hide their limits. Here are ours.

  • No AI on your records.Kinsu doesn’t run AI on your health data, and doesn’t send it anywhere to be analysed.
  • No sharing, yet.Nobody else can open your records: not your family, not your doctor, not us. To show a doctor, you show them your phone.
  • No cloud backup.Records live only on your phone. If you lose the phone, they go with it, and they can’t be recovered, by you or by us.
  • Not a medical device.Kinsu doesn’t diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Your doctor does.
  • No ads, and nothing sold.The app has no advertising and no analytics, and nothing you put in it is ever sold.

Questions

Questions, answered honestly.

What does it cost?

Kinsu is free to download, and has no ads.

Is my data sent to servers?

Your records aren’t. Everything you add stays on your phone, encrypted. Of what you put in Kinsu, two things leave it: your mobile number, which Google Firebase uses to text you a sign-in code, and an encrypted copy of your recovery key, which only your 12-word code can open. Signing in also sends Firebase technical details, such as your IP address and device identifiers, that it needs to sign you in safely.

Can my family see my records?

No. Your records, and the ones you keep for family members, stay on your phone. Nobody else can open them, and Kinsu doesn’t send them to anyone.

Can I move my records to a new phone?

Not yet. Records live only on the phone you added them on. You can export the categories you choose as spreadsheet files, but Kinsu can’t import them back. The files aren’t encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

What if I lose my phone?

Your records are only on your phone, so they go with it: there’s no cloud copy to restore from. Whoever has the phone can’t open them without your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Your 12-word recovery code gets you back in on the same phone if its security settings change; it can’t bring records to a new one.

Why Android only?

Kinsu is Android-only for now, because that’s where we’re starting. There’s no iPhone version yet.

Does it give medical advice?

No. Kinsu is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Always ask your doctor.

Is Kinsu connected to ABHA?

No. Kinsu doesn’t connect to ABHA, ABDM or any national health record system.
