Free printables

Free logs and checklists you can print today

No sign-up and no email. Print a sheet, or save the image to your phone. They’re blank forms: you write the numbers, and your doctor says what they mean.

Pick one, print it, use it this week.

Using them well

A few honest notes.

The sheets only record what you write. They don’t say what a number means or what to do about it. Ask your doctor how often to measure and which numbers suit you.

You’re welcome to copy and share them, for free, as they are. If you keep readings in the Kinsu app instead, you can export them as a spreadsheet file to show your doctor.
